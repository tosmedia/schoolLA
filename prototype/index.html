Maryland Family Child Care is a licensed home on N Maryland Avenue, run by Anna Poghosyan.

We collect your child from school ourselves, keep them until you arrive. On days school is out, by arrangement two days ahead.

California caps a home this size at six children, ages 5 to 13 — so six is the most there will ever be.

Questions parents actually ask

Answers, in plain language.

No. This is a licensed family child care home. Your child goes to their own school; we take them from the last bell until you collect them. Days when school is closed are by arrangement, agreed with Anna two days ahead. Enrolling here changes nothing about where your child is enrolled for school.

Yes. We collect your child from school ourselves, by car, and bring them back to the house. Call or text us with your child's school and we'll say straight away whether it works.

Afternoons 12:00 to 6:00 p.m., from the last bell until you collect them.

We collect children from school; we do not take them there in the morning.

Six o'clock is a real deadline, not a suggestion — it is when Anna's own day ends.

Days when school is closed — winter break, spring break, summer and in-service days — work by arrangement. If you would like to leave your child with Anna on one of those days, agree it with her at least two days in advance, by phone or text.

Help. A full hour is set aside at 4:00, and with six children one adult can sit down next to each of them. Most children leave with it finished. If your child is stuck on the same thing week after week, you will hear it from us before you hear it from school.

The house is licensed by the California Department of Social Services, Community Care Licensing Division. Ask us for the licence number — anyone can then look up the facility, its inspection history and any complaints on the department's public site. We would rather you did.
